From 6th to 10th October 2021, 27 youth workers from Croatia, Estonia, Germany, Greece, Italy, Poland, Portugal and Spain met in Giovinazzo (near Bari, Italy) to explore non formal education and the soft skills most required by the labour market. This video is just a taste of what they lived.
