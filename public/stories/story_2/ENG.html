From 30th May to 6th June 2024, Ecotopia, a mobility of youth workers, was held in the town of Giovinazzo, Bari, Italy. The course was co-financed by the European Union's Erasmus+ programme and was attended by 34 youth workers from 10 countries: Italy, Spain, Belgium, Greece, Poland, Portugal, Morocco, Georgia, Azerbaijan and Turkey.

The main objective of the project was to encourage young people to become active citizens able to play a key role in the sustainable development of their communities and to effect environmental changes in their societies.

During the project the participants learnt new methodologies and tools that they will now be able to pass on, enabling young people not only to be more aware of environmental problems, but also to become proactive advocates.

After the end of the course, the participants will continue to meet in national groups for disseminating the results of the projects while organising environmental actions.

This project has been funded with support from the European Commission.
