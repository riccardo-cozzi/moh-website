Privacy and cookie policy

Last updated: 1 August 2026

This notice describes how the website of MOH – Mobility Opportunities Happening uses data and local technologies.

Cookies and tracking tools

This website does not use its own profiling cookies, advertising cookies or traffic analytics tools. Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Meta Pixel and similar systems are not installed.

Language preference storage

The website uses localStorage only to remember the language selected by the user. It stores only a language code and does not identify the user.

External services and links

The website contains links to social networks, sharing platforms and partner websites. When users open these links, they leave this website and data processing is governed by the respective providers’ policies.

Contact

The contact form prepares a mailto: message and opens the user’s email client. The data is therefore processed by the relevant email provider under its own terms and policies.

Hosting

The website is published through GitHub Pages. Any technical data processed by the hosting service is subject to GitHub’s policies.

This page is provided for information and should be reviewed and updated by the data controller according to the applicable law.
