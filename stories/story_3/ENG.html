9 days, a training course, 36 youth workers from 11 different coastal countries and islands reunited to find new strategies to solve the huge plastic pollution problem. The project WTP! "WHAT THE PLASTIC!" [2021-1-IT03-KA153-YOU-000011938] has been a gentle way to intend the WTF we usually say aloud when we see our beaches and rivers and streets polluted by plastics.

We had fun, we cleaned up beaches, we created and recycled, we devised new strategies to minimize a problem which is huge, but above all, we lived together and enjoyed this project very much!
