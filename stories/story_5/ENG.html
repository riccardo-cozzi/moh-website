From 13th to 19th October 2021, 24 youth workers from Croatia, Estonia, France, Italy, Poland, Spain and Turkey met in Giovinazzo (near Bari, Italy) to explore non formal education, wellbeing, soft skills and self development. It was a chance for them to challenge themselves and explore new learning possibilities.

The training course has been funded by the Erasmus+ Programme of the European Union.

The project was organised by the Italian organisation MOH - Mobility Opportunities Happening APS.

Partner Organisations:

  • Euromuevete - Spain
  • Fundacja MODE - Move and Develop Foundation - Poland
  • Udruga Prizma - Croatia
  • MITRA France - France
  • Eesti People to People - Estonia
  • Aksaray Universitesi Uluslararası Öğrenci Kulübü - Turkey
